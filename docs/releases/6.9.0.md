# Rigour 6.9.0

A change is checked against the goal its pull request declares, and Rigour keeps a record of what happened after each
merge. Both are off until a team or a person turns them on.

## New

- **The goal check** ([GOAL.md](../GOAL.md)). The pull request's `Scope`, `Out of scope` and `Done when` (also
  `Acceptance criteria`, `Definition of done`, `Success criteria`) are read from its description, and the change is
  checked against them without a model:
  - a changed file outside the scope, or inside out of scope, blocks;
  - a `Done when` item naming a file the change never touches blocks;
  - a named symbol the change never touches is a note.

  It runs in `rigour review`, before an agent stops, and before a push, where it reads the branch's open pull request.
  With the reviewer on, the judge also says whether each prose item and invariant is met. An item that isn't met is a
  should-fix, never a block. The switch is `review.goal`, `RIGOUR_GOAL`, `goal` in your settings, or `--goal`.
- **The outcome record** ([OUTCOMES.md](../OUTCOMES.md)). `rigour outcomes` keeps one record per merged pull request:
  - CI on its merge commit, counting only a check the merge broke;
  - the later commits on its files within the window, and which of them say they fix something;
  - a revert.

  A record settles once its window has closed and CI has an answer, and is then never read again. The switch is
  `learning.outcomes.mode`, `RIGOUR_OUTCOMES`, `outcomes` in your settings, or `--outcomes`.
- **Lessons and outcomes.** With the outcome loop on:
  - a later fix on a review point's own lines is shown in Studio for a person to promote or dismiss;
  - a lesson that recurrence promoted is taken back to a candidate when later pull requests, which a review found
    repeating it, merged anyway and stayed fine;
  - a person's decision is final.
- **Studio's Setup page** has one control per switch (the goal check, the outcome loop): what runs, which layer
  decided, yours and the team's. Team changes are written to `rigour.yml` for you to commit.

## Changed

- **Lessons promoted only by a later fix on the same lines are back to candidate; review them in Studio.** That signal
  no longer promotes a lesson on its own: a person read it against real history and found most such fixes were unrelated
  work. Each one keeps all its evidence and gains a `reclassified` record saying why. Studio lists them first, with
  Promote and Dismiss. Lessons promoted by recurrence, by a correction or by a person are unchanged.

## Upgrade

`npm install -g @rigour-labs/cli@latest`. Nothing to migrate: the lessons file is updated the next time Rigour writes it.
